AI ASSISTANCE HISTORY
=====================

Project: Smart Parking Lot Rate Calculator
Purpose: Coding assignment for Sahaj

1. Problem Understanding
------------------------
Reviewed the parking-rate requirements and identified the main domain concepts:

- ParkingSession
- VehicleType
- LoyaltyTier
- Standard Hourly pricing
- Early Bird pricing
- Night Owl pricing
- Peak-hour pricing
- Vehicle multipliers
- Loyalty discounts
- Best-value policy selection

The implementation was designed as a small, platform-agnostic Python application.

2. Design Approach
-------------------
Used a policy-based design to separate pricing rules.

Main abstraction:

    PricingPolicy
        |
        +-- StandardHourlyPolicy
        +-- EarlyBirdPolicy
        +-- NightOwlPolicy

ParkingRateCalculator evaluates all applicable policies and returns
the minimum valid fare.

Reusable pricing concerns such as vehicle multipliers, loyalty
discounts, and peak-hour detection remain separated from the
individual pricing policies.

The design intentionally avoids unnecessary frameworks, databases,
APIs, or infrastructure because they are outside the assignment scope.

3. Domain Models
----------------
Implemented domain models for:

- VehicleType
- LoyaltyTier
- ParkingSession

ParkingSession validates that the exit timestamp occurs after the
entry timestamp.

4. Pricing Components
---------------------
Implemented reusable components for:

- Vehicle-specific pricing multipliers
- Loyalty discounts
- Peak-hour detection

Decimal was used for monetary calculations to avoid floating-point
precision issues.

5. Standard Hourly Policy
-------------------------
Implemented progressive hourly pricing:

- First hour: $5
- Second hour: $3
- Subsequent hours: $2

Partial hours are rounded up.

Hourly blocks start from the exact parking entry timestamp.

Each hourly block is evaluated independently for peak-hour overlap.

Weekday peak windows:

- 07:00-10:00
- 16:00-19:00

Peak multiplier:

- 1.5x

The assignment example was explicitly tested:

06:30 -> 08:30 = $12.00

6. Special Policies
--------------------
Implemented Early Bird:

- Entry: 06:00 inclusive to 09:00 exclusive
- Exit: 15:30 inclusive to 19:00 exclusive
- Same calendar day
- Maximum 24-hour stay
- Base fare: $15

Implemented Night Owl:

- Entry: 18:00 inclusive to midnight
- Exit: 05:00 inclusive to 10:00 exclusive
  on the following calendar day
- Maximum 24-hour stay
- Base fare: $8

Vehicle multipliers and loyalty discounts are applied to special
policy fares.

7. Best Value Logic
-------------------
Implemented ParkingRateCalculator to:

1. Evaluate every configured pricing policy.
2. Ignore policies that are not applicable.
3. Select the minimum valid fare.

The calculator accepts an optional list of policies, making it easier
to test and extend without modifying the calculator itself.

9. Engineering Assumptions
---------------------------
The assignment specifies public-holiday exclusions from peak pricing
but does not provide a holiday calendar or data source.

Therefore, the implementation treats Monday-Friday as weekdays and
Saturday-Sunday as non-peak days. No external holiday dependency was
introduced.

Timestamps are treated as local parking-lot timestamps because the
assignment does not define timezone conversion requirements.
